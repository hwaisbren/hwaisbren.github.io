My November is a Boston-based four-piece emo band delivering heartfelt, guitar-driven anthems inspired by the likes of ‘90s Jimmy Eat World, Title Fight, Have Heart, and Lifetime. Formed by guitarist and vocalist Henry Waisbren, bassist Michael Watters, drummer Garrett Briskin, and later joined by guitarist Mikey Boylan, the band channels the raw emotion and melodic intensity that defined the golden era of emo while injecting their own modern edge.

Band History

My November was officially formed in December 2024 after Henry and Michael had talked about starting a band enough times and decided to actually do it. Originally just a side project, they asked Garrett to record drums on the EP they were working on. After working on the project for a while, the band released its self-titled EP on July 7th, 2025. The band decided to do a one off release show on October 4th, but it went so well they decided to keep playing consistently. The band then recruited Mikey to join as an official guitarist after playing with him as a live member consistently. After writing more songs over the course of the next year, the band released its second EP, “Leave It All Behind”, on July 25, 2026.
