About

My November is an emo band from Boston delivering heartfelt, guitar-driven songs inspired by the likes of ‘90s Jimmy Eat World, Title Fight, Have Heart, and Lifetime. Formed in 2024, They have released two EPs, "My November" on July 7, 2025 and "Leave It All Behind" on July 25, 2026.
