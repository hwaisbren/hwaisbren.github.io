My November is a Boston-based four-piece band delivering heartfelt, guitar-driven songs inspired by the likes of ‘90s Jimmy Eat World, Title Fight, Have Heart, and Lifetime. Formed by guitarist and vocalist Henry Waisbren, bassist Michael Watters, drummer Garrett Briskin and were later joined by guitarist Mikey Boylan. My November was officially formed in December 2024 after Henry and Michael had talked about starting a band enough times and decided to actually do it. Originally just a side project, they asked Garrett to record drums on the EP they were working on. On July 7th, 2025, the band had its first release: a self-titled EP. A one-off release show was held on October 4th, but it went so well they decided to keep playing consistently. The band then recruited Mikey to join as an official guitarist after playing with him as a live member consistently. Over the course of the next year, the band continued to write new music and eventually released its second EP, “Leave It All Behind”, on July 25, 2026.
